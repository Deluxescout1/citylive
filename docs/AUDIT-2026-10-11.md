fix any and all bugs found." Branch: `feat/rain-front-water-flow` (rain-on-front-water feature + audit fixes).

3. **Life-walk** → kept as `desktop/test/life-walk.test.js` (~18 s): the complement to the matrix.

## Bugs found and fixed (one commit each)
1. **DST-correction drift for New-World restarts** — `lifeIndexOf` read the timezone offset at
   `w = now + WORLD_SHIFT`; the Micah identity knob (`-lives*GROW_CYCLE`, up to ~11 *years* of whole
   cycles) threw `w` years off the real date, so the correction read a synthetic winter offset and
   moved a chosen 20:00 rollover to 21:00 for most hours of the day. Fix: the correction reads
   `offMs(now)` — what its comment always claimed. Regression: `apoc-hour.test.js` walks restarts
   applied at *every* wall hour.
2. **fennec NaN** — `FAUNA.fennec` had no `w/h`, but the `PREDATORS` hunter table reads them raw:
   dunes and salt painted ~225 NaN `fillRect`s per sweep — the hunt scene never rendered at all.
   Silent-zero, the "ninth species declared but did not exist" class. Fix: gave fennec dimensions.
   Guarded by render-matrix (`bad === 0` across the matrix).
3. **el-train pantograph never rendered** — `cwY` (catenary height) was declared inside
   `drawTrainLine`'s `part!=="service"` gate, but the pantograph runs in the `"service"` call:
   every service pass executed `fillRect(x, NaN, …)` (silently ignored). Moved to the top.
   Guarded by life-walk (trains only run late in the growth curve — the matrix couldn't see it).
4. **weekly side-system dead** — unit `ExecStart` and `timelapse.sh` scraped `GROW_CYCLE=(\d+)`
   out of the engine source; it stopped being a literal (`cycleMs(CFG.cycle)`) when config cycles
   arrived, so the scrape returned null, the service failed every run, chronicle.json went stale
   for ~12 weeks and no timelapse rendered past life-0. Fix: `tools/lifeindex.js` asks the engine
   (vm load, same trick the suite uses); unit + `timelapse.sh` delegate to it. Guarded by
   `weekly-side-system.test.js` — THE CLOCK NUMBERS MUST COME FROM THE ENGINE, NEVER A SCRAPE.

## Rules this audit added (generalize the two failure classes above)
- The canvas NaN classes are *three*, not one: coordinates, **gradient args**, and **style
  strings** (`"rgba(…,NaN)"` paints nothing and throws nothing). Any canvas harness must patrol
  all three.
- A "blank" pass is not necessarily a bug: `sky/skyfast/cloud` early-return by design, and a
  front-water land legally zeroes its seam bay (`SEA_FRONT>0 ⇒ seaW=0` — "two coastlines read as
  nowhere"); a seam ocean is also frequently just *not visible from this slice* (seams live near
  the world edges). Blank-assert only when the geometry actually should reach the slice.
- Anything that runs "later in the life" (trains, disasters, endings) needs a walk test, not a
  frozen-instant matrix slot.
- Side systems rot silently: their failure mode is a systemd oneshot that "ran" — drive them by
  hand and assert the outputs, or the weekly dying for 12 weeks is invisible.
